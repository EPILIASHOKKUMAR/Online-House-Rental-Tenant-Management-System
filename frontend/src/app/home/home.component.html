One Platform. Three Experiences.

Built for real people.

person

Tenant Access

Tenants can only browse properties, submit booking requests, and view the status of their own requests. They cannot access owner data or system-level information.

real_estate_agent

Owner Access

Owners can manage only the properties they have listed and respond to booking requests related to those properties. They cannot view or modify other owners' data.

admin_panel_settings

Admin Access

Administrators have system-level access to monitor users, properties, and platform activity. All administrative actions are logged and restricted to authorized accounts.
